- Route ids are 6 characters: a 2-letter prefix (CR, FM) and a 4-digit zero-padded number.
- Measures are in miles from the route's origin, to 3 decimal places.
- Recalibration happens quarterly; events located before a recalibration may shift up to 0.01 mile.
